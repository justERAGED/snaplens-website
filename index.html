Features

Everything you need

A complete AI assistant that works with whatever is on your screen.

📸

One-click screenshots

A tiny floating dot sits in your corner. Click it. SnapLens captures what's on screen and sends it to AI instantly.

🎓

Exam mode

Detects MCQ, short-answer, and fill-in-the-blank questions. Gives you the right answer with a clear explanation.

🌐

Your language

Set any language and all answers come back in it — no matter what language the content on screen is in.

🖼️

Understands images too

Charts, diagrams, photos — not just text. The AI describes and interprets anything visible on your screen.

⚡

Instant answers

Responses appear right where the dot was. No new tabs, no context switching. The square expands to fit the answer.

🎛️

Fully customizable

Control answer length, AI mode, dot position, and dot size — all adjustable from the extension popup anytime.
